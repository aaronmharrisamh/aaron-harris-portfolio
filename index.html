Forerunner 3

HarrisXR LLC, in collaboration with Space Interactions Inc. · Technical Lead · 2025–Present

A web-based 3D visualization platform, the "Google Earth" for satellites: it renders every object from low Earth orbit to geostationary against live Google Earth terrain, accurate and fast on phone and desktop alike.

Built by HarrisXR LLC in collaboration with Space Interactions Inc. My brother and I lead the development: a fast-tracked, AI-augmented rebuild that migrated a pure WebGL 2.0 renderer onto modern BabylonJS and TypeScript. I own the architecture, UI, and design direction while driving much of the implementation through Claude Code and Codex, marshalling both to ship in weeks what would traditionally take months. The result holds proper SGP4 propagation and real geopositioning accuracy while rendering 30,000+ tracked objects at a smooth clip on mid-range phones.

    Space Interactions Inc. · Technical Lead · Feb 2024–2025

    A low-spec, self-hostable space-domain awareness platform: jump to any date and see the full satellite picture for that moment, running smoothly on hardware as light as a Surface Pro.

              About

              I'm Aaron Harris, a technical lead and solutions architect who has been shipping production software since 2011. I started building games and tools when I was nine, and that lifelong systems instinct still shows up in how I architect, debug, and lead today.

              My range is end-to-end: full-stack systems, UI/UX, simulation, integrations, AI-assisted workflows, and the judgment to know what should be built, delegated, simplified, or protected from technical debt.
